# 六如

Liuru --- "as-if, as-if, as-if, as-if, as-if, as-if"

---

## A name he chose, not one he was born with

In his early thirties, fresh off his scandal and money troubles, Tang Yin
took the Buddhist name **Liuru Jushi**, "the Layman of the Six As-ifs" ---
and kept it for the two decades left to him.

It comes from the closing lines of the Diamond Sutra:

> All conditioned things are as a dream, an illusion, a bubble, a shadow,
> dew, lightning.

---

{/* _class: centered */}

## Six images, one claim

- a dream
- an illusion
- a bubble
- a shadow
- dew
- lightning

Each looks like something solid, for exactly as long as you don't look
closely.

---

## The usual reading

Personal piety. A painter past his scandals, finding a philosophy that
fits his circumstances --- impermanence, illusion, the wisdom of letting go.

Nothing in that reading has anything to do with painting.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> A painter whose posthumous identity is mostly made of things that only
> look like what they claim to be, choosing, in his own lifetime, a name
> that says exactly that about everything.

---

## What this course has spent ten weeks asking

"Which one is the real Tang Yin?"

Materials, brushwork, seals, colophons, the market, computation --- every
week built another instrument for answering that one question.

---

## What the Diamond Sutra actually says

Not "these things are fake, look past them to what's real underneath."

The error isn't the illusion. **The error is demanding that something stop
being illusion-like and become solid.**

---

## The uncomfortable fit

A field built entirely around "but which one is the real one" is asking a
question its own subject's chosen name already answered ---

and not in the field's favour.

---

{/* _class: centered */}

## This week doesn't resolve the irony

It sits in it, on purpose.

---

## What does change

If "authentic" is a useful working distinction, not a fact waiting to be
uncovered underneath the evidence, does anything the last ten weeks taught
you stop being useful?

(No. It changes what you think you're doing with it.)

---

## What this doesn't let you off the hook for

The final project still asks for a **defended verdict**, not a shrug.

Six as-ifs is a stance on what kind of thing "authentic" is --- not a
licence to stop arguing about which paintings get the name.
