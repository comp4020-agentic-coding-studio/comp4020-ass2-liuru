Wren builds stroke-level classifiers for ink-and-brush painting --- the kind
of model that has learned to tell two hands apart from how a line tapers, not
from anything a human viewer could easily name. Wren guest-teaches week 9 on
that work and is exact about what it is good for: a second opinion that never
gets tired or flattered, not a verdict, and one that inherits every bias in
whatever confirmed attributions it was trained on.

Wren's own view, stated on the first slide every year: a model that agrees
with the connoisseurs is not yet interesting, and a model that disagrees with
them is not yet trustworthy. The interesting cases are the ones sitting
between those two sentences, and week 9 spends its whole ninety minutes there.
